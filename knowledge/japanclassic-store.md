# Japan Classic — Store Knowledge Base

## About
Japan Classic is an online store selling authentic Japanese handmade ceramics to international customers. Every piece is one-of-a-kind, crafted by skilled Japanese artisans. Nothing is mass-produced.

## Shipping
- Ships from Japan via EMS (Express Mail Service) by Japan Post
- Most orders arrive within two weeks
- Shipping insurance is fully covered by Japan Classic at no extra cost
- Tracking is available for all orders
- Shipping fees are separate from product prices to keep base prices as low as possible

## Returns & Refunds
- Contact within one week of receiving your order for a full refund
- Return shipping costs are covered by Japan Classic
- Shipping insurance covers damage during transit

## Product Care
- All items are handmade ceramics
- Hand washing recommended
- Not microwave safe unless specifically noted
- Not dishwasher safe — hand wash only
- Each piece may have slight variations due to handmade nature — this is a feature, not a defect

## Payment
- Prices are in USD
- Accepts major credit cards via Shopify checkout

## Brand Values
- Sharing Japanese craftsmanship with the world
- Offering the most reasonable prices possible for authentic artisan work
- Every piece tells a story of Japanese tradition and skill

## Common Customer Questions
Q: How long does shipping take?
A: Most orders arrive within two weeks via EMS from Japan.

Q: Is shipping insured?
A: Yes, all shipments are fully insured at no extra cost to you.

Q: Can I return an item?
A: Yes, contact us within one week of receiving your order for a full refund. We cover return shipping costs.

Q: Are these mass-produced?
A: No. Every piece is handmade by a professional Japanese ceramic artist. Each item is one-of-a-kind.

Q: Why are shipping fees separate?
A: We keep product prices as low as possible by not bundling shipping costs. This way you see the true price of the craftsmanship.

Q: Can I use these in the microwave?
A: Hand washing is recommended and microwave use is not advised unless specifically noted on the product page.

Q: What if my item arrives damaged?
A: All shipments are insured. Contact us and we will arrange a full refund or replacement.

Q: Can I request a custom piece?
A: Depending on the artist and piece, we may be able to request additional production. Please contact us to discuss.
